🎨 Voice Vibes

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Voice Vibes ("the Game", "we", "us") is a global multiplayer drawing/guessing activity playable inside Discord or in a web browser. This policy explains what information the Game collects and how it is used. By playing the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Discord identity (when playing via Discord): your Discord user ID, username, and avatar — used only to display your name, avatar, and score on the in-game leaderboard.
  • Channel ID (when playing via Discord): the ID of the voice channel hosting the activity — used to identify the current game session.
  • Guest identity (when playing in a browser): a randomly generated ID stored in your browser's local storage, so your score can follow you between sessions.
  • Game data: your guesses, guess timing, drawings, and score, which are stored in a Firebase Realtime Database to run the game and leaderboard.

2. How We Use the Data

  • To operate the game: show everyone the same drawing at the same time, score guesses, and rank players on a shared persistent leaderboard.
  • To let your score persist: if you leave and return, your score and rank are restored.

3. Data Storage & Retention

Game data is stored on Firebase Realtime Database servers (operated by Google LLC). Scores persist so returning players keep their progress. You can request deletion of your data at any time via the contact details below, and we will remove your player record from the leaderboard.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Discord Inc. — the embedded app SDK and OAuth provide your Discord identity for login. Discord's own privacy policy applies to your Discord account.
  • Google Firebase — hosts game state (players, scores, answers, questions).
  • Cloudflare — hosts and serves the Game itself.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We do not read Discord messages or channel content.

5. Children

The Game is a general-audience drawing activity and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
